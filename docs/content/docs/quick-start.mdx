## Self-Hosted

### 1. Install the server

Follow [Installing Scanopy (Self-Hosted)](/self-hosted-server/server-installation/). The Docker Compose install is the quickest path. On a commercial plan, follow [Deploying the Commercial Edition](/self-hosted-server/commercial-deployment/) instead, which adds your license key.

### 2. Complete onboarding

Open your server URL. The onboarding wizard walks you through initial setup.

### 3. Wait for your first scan

Docker Compose deployments include an **integrated daemon** that automatically begins scanning your network after onboarding. The first scan is a light scan that discovers hosts and open ports. A typical /24 subnet takes around 30 minutes depending on the number of active hosts. Watch progress in real-time on the **Discovery** page.

### 4. Explore your network

- **Topology**: interactive diagrams of your infrastructure in four views
- **Assets > Hosts**: all discovered devices
- **Assets > Services**: detected services across your network

To scan additional networks, deploy daemons on other segments. See [Planning Daemon Deployment](/setting-up-daemons/planning-daemon-deployment/).

## Cloud

Scanopy Cloud runs the server for you.

### 1. Create your account

Open the Cloud tab on the [pricing page](https://scanopy.net/pricing) and pick a plan. The onboarding wizard guides you through creating your organization, your first network, and your account, then asks you to choose your plan.

### 2. Install a daemon

After onboarding, you'll be prompted to install a **daemon**. A daemon is a lightweight agent that runs on your network, discovers hosts and services, and reports findings to the server. Follow the install command shown in the modal for your platform, or see [Managing Daemons](/setting-up-daemons/managing-daemons/) for details.

### 3. Run your first scan

Once the daemon connects, go to **Discover > Scan > Scheduled** and click **Run** on the discovery. A typical /24 subnet takes around 30 minutes depending on the number of active hosts. You can watch progress in real-time on the **Discovery** page.

### 4. Explore your network

The same views as [Explore your network](#4-explore-your-network) in the self-hosted steps.
