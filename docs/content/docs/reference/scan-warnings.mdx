---
title: Scan Warnings
description: What each scan warning means, grouped by what it asks you to do.
---

A completed scan session can carry warnings. Each one is filed under what it asks of you: fix something in Scanopy, check the device, wait for a scan that clears it, or nothing. Its colour and icon show how much the scan lost.
