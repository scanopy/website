---
title: Scan Warnings
description: What each scan warning means, grouped by what it asks you to do.
---

A completed scan session can carry warnings. Each one is filed under what it asks of you — fix something in Scanopy, go check the device, wait for a scan that will clear it on its own, or nothing at all — and separately marked by colour and icon for how much the scan lost, which is not always the same thing: a credential failure is red even though the fix is a few clicks, and a permanently malformed record can ask nothing of you while still being red.
