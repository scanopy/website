The topology generates an interactive visualization of your infrastructure, showing hosts, services, subnets, and their relationships. You can view your infrastructure from four different perspectives, each answering a different question.

## Perspectives

Each perspective shows a different angle of your infrastructure:

- **[L2 Physical](/using-scanopy/topology/l2-physical)** — How is my network physically wired?
- **[L3 Logical](/using-scanopy/topology/l3-logical)** — How is my network logically segmented?
- **[Workloads](/using-scanopy/topology/workloads)** — Where does everything run, and what's inside what?
- **[Application](/using-scanopy/topology/application)** — How do my services depend on each other?

Each perspective has its own grouping options and edge types.

## Live View and Snapshots

The **Live** view always reflects your current discovery data — as scans add, change, or remove entities, the topology updates to match.

To preserve a point-in-time view of your infrastructure, capture a **snapshot**. Snapshots are read-only and don't change as discovery continues, so you can compare your network against a past state. See [Snapshots](/using-scanopy/topology/snapshots/).

## Refining Your Topology

After initial discovery, these refinements make your topology more accurate across all perspectives.

### Assign Unclaimed Ports

Open a host and use **Unclaimed Open Ports** on its **Services** tab to assign detected ports to the services that are actually listening on them.

See [Unclaimed Open Ports](/using-scanopy/network-data/#unclaimed-open-ports).

### Consolidate Multi-NIC Hosts

Hosts detected across multiple VLANs can be merged into a single logical host.

See [Consolidating Duplicate Hosts](/using-scanopy/network-data/#consolidating-duplicate-hosts).

## Layout Is Automatic

Scanopy positions every node and connection for you. To change what the map shows, use [grouping rules and filters](/using-scanopy/topology/customizing-views/). To change how much detail it shows, use the expand/collapse control at the top right, or press `]` and `[`, to step the whole diagram between fully expanded and fully collapsed.

## Exporting

Click **Export** to download the current view as it's drawn, with your filters, collapsed containers and layout intact. PNG, SVG, PDF and HTML give you a picture for a report. draw.io and Visio (`.vsdx`) give you an editable diagram: every container, card and connection is a native shape you can rearrange, restyle or annotate, so you can finish a network diagram for a customer handover or an audit without redrawing it. Mermaid and Confluence give you wiki markup. Which formats are available depends on your plan.
