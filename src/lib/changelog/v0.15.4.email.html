Scanopy just got more intuitive and robust, making network discovery smoother for busy environments and mobile workflows. This release tackles reliability, performance, and clarity so you can explore your network with confidence.

  • Reduce scan confusion: Configurable ARP scan limits and new, inline warnings ensure you always know what’s happening during large subnet scans.
  • Smoother Docker integration: Improved host persistence and smarter deduplication when scanning Docker bridge networks means fewer scan interruptions and more accurate results.
  • Faster topology exports: Service logos are now bundled locally for quicker loading and reliable map downloads, even when exporting from mobile devices.
  • Mobile usability upgrades: Enhanced onboarding, full-width buttons, and refined dropdowns make network mapping easier on the go.
  • Clearer error guidance: Improved connection error messages help you pinpoint and resolve network reachability issues faster.
