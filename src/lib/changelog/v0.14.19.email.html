This Scanopy update focuses on making onboarding smoother and network discovery more intuitive, so you can start mapping your environment with less effort and more clarity.

  • Simplified onboarding: Brand new guided setup checklist and smarter empty states help you track progress and get oriented from your first login.
  • Clearer network previews: Interactive mini topology with scan animations and smoother service hover highlights give instant visual feedback on your network status.
  • Easier scan controls: Enable or pause recurring discoveries with dashboard toggles - no need to dig through settings.
  • Real-time host updates: Watch newly discovered devices appear during scans for faster troubleshooting and confidence.
  • Improved setup & troubleshooting: Redesigned daemon install flows and clearer connection/error messaging reduce confusion and help resolve issues faster.
