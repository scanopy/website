We're excited to announce a new Scanopy release focused on making discovery simpler, setup clearer, and scanning more reliable for your lab and network environments.

  • Launch scans with less guesswork: A reorganized Discovery modal streamlines options and tabs, so you can kick off scans and configure settings more intuitively.
  • Less confusion on first install: A centralized daemon setup prompt keeps new organization rollouts moving and prevents missed setup steps.
  • More predictable scans: Improved scan progress estimation and smarter Docker probe retries mean scans are both more accurate and resilient to hiccups.
  • Snappier demo environments: Faster demo data management helps you try features and reset labs with minimal downtime during tests.
  • Fewer scan interruptions: Fixes for endpoint hangs and Docker proxy handling ensure all endpoints (including non-HTTP) are scanned reliably every time.

These updates aim to give you a more streamlined, error-free topology mapping workflow - whether you're onboarding new sites or exploring your network's full layout.
