We're excited to share a new Scanopy update focused on keeping your network mapping smooth and reliable. This release tackles common pain points around licensing and database upgrades.

  • 7-day license grace period: Avoid unexpected interruptions — you now have extra time to resolve license issues before Scanopy services are affected.
  • Resilient topology labels: Device alias entries are preserved during database upgrades, so your custom labels stay intact across baselines.
  • Streamlined Docker deployments: Standard OCI labels are now included, improving compatibility with registries and automated setups.
  • Improved reliability: Eliminated a rare race condition that could affect deployment event reporting, making Scanopy even more robust.

These enhancements make ongoing management, customizations, and upgrades easier and safer for your network environment.
