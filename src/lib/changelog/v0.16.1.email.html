We’ve just released a Scanopy update designed to make managing and sharing your network topology faster and more secure. This release focuses on helping you control map visibility and collaborate confidently, especially in larger or more complex environments.

  • Granular filtering controls: Quickly show or hide services, ports, and metadata for precise map views, reducing clutter and focusing on what matters most.
  • Secure topology sharing: Easily grant temporary access to network maps with password protection and access tokens—collaborate without compromising security.
  • Clearer map starting points: Smart collapsed sections in the default L3 topology view let you navigate big networks without being overwhelmed.
  • Smoother UI interactions: Refined filter panel feedback and improved warning displays make management more intuitive and alerts easier to spot.
  • Better map readability: Node overlaps and service card issues are resolved, so live topology views remain accurate and clear as your network changes.
