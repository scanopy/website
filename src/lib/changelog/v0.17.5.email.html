We've just released new improvements to Scanopy, focused on eliminating common pain points and making network discovery workflows more intuitive.

  • Effortless daemon management: Handle install, configuration, and legacy keys from one unified modal—no more juggling separate workflows or losing track of key details.
  • Quickly spot stale network components: Stale tags are now consistently surfaced across digest, inventory, and topology maps, so you can prioritize cleanup and stay current.
  • Simplified daemon install and edit: Fewer setup steps, smarter wizard flow, and always-accessible, environment-specific install commands to make onboarding new daemons a breeze.
  • Accurate container network mapping: Containers visually connect to all subnets and their host bridges, giving you a clearer picture of your Docker environments.
  • Reliable multi-daemon installs: Improved concurrency and scan stability remove past issues when provisioning multiple daemons on real hosts.

Scanopy continues to help you visualize, manage, and maintain your networks with less friction and more clarity.
