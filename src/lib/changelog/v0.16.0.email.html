The latest Scanopy update brings sweeping improvements to how you visualize and explore your network. Expect a smoother mapping experience, more intuitive navigation, and a refreshed look throughout the app.

  • Clearer topology maps: Complex relationships are much easier to understand with new entity-aware labels and improved dependency edges.
  • Smarter map navigation: The fitView function now frames large topologies without node overlap, making it effortless to keep your view organized.
  • Speedier search and exploration: Find devices and services even inside collapsed containers, with direct navigation to highlighted results.
  • Improved dependency mapping: Visualize application and service links with sentence-style cards, entity tagging, and clearer edge types.
  • Cleaner, consistent visuals: Updated icons and palettes make every corner of Scanopy easier on the eyes and more consistent to use.
