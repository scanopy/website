This release focuses on more easily diagnosable scan stalls - you'll be able to see why a scan ended, and what went wrong.

## New Features

- Add an Issues tab that collects everything that went wrong during a scan in one place, with a one-click copy of a failed run's diagnostics for support.
- Show why a scan ended — completed, cancelled, or failed — and warn you while it's running if it stops reporting progress or degrades, instead of leaving it spinning indefinitely.

## Improvements

- Send a scan its subnets up front, removing a round trip that could delay discovery starting.

## Bug Fixes

- Fix scans that could hang forever when a daemon disappeared, was busy, or refused a cancellation — sessions now fail cleanly and stop the daemon.
- Fix probes and senders continuing to run after a scan was cancelled.
- Fix newer daemons being misidentified as legacy versions.
