We've made Scanopy easier and more intuitive with improved credential handling and reminders, authentication clarity, and usability fixes. Here’s how this release helps streamline your network mapping workflows:

  • Simpler Docker scan setup: Consolidated proxy instructions in the credential wizard reduce confusion and let you start local Docker scans faster.
  • Clearer sign-in experience: New login method selector and better error messages mean fewer authentication hiccups, especially with OIDC providers.
  • Safer topology management: Accidental deletion of your last topology is prevented, ensuring your maps always stay protected.
  • Smoother onboarding: Discovery flows and navigation after first setup are now more reliable, minimizing stalls and missteps.
  • Visual feedback improvements: Enhanced test results, toast messages, and layout tweaks make in-app actions feel more responsive and readable.
