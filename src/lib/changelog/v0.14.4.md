## New Features

- Add Free tier and overhaul billing system with improved onboarding, making it simpler for users to get started and manage their subscriptions ([#491](https://github.com/scanopy/scanopy/pull/491)).
- Add referral source and Cloud-only qualification fields to help tailor the onboarding experience and streamline account setup ([#494](https://github.com/scanopy/scanopy/pull/494)).

## Improvements

- Replace HubSpot CRM and transactional email integration with Brevo for streamlined account communications and improved reliability ([#491](https://github.com/scanopy/scanopy/pull/491)).
- Update billing lifecycle handling for failed payments, payment method changes, and real-time subscription status, providing clearer feedback during account changes.
- Enhance UI with billing tooltips and centralized plan restrictions for more intuitive subscription upgrades and management.
