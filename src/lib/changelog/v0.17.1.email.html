This Scanopy update streamlines key setup tasks and improves visibility, making it even easier to manage your network and agents. We’ve focused on reducing configuration friction and clarifying device information.

  • Unified credential and agent setup: Save time with a single wizard that guides you through adding, naming, and assigning both network and daemon credentials.
  • Precision credential assignments: Assign credentials to specific hosts or networks, with live visibility of where they apply—no more guessing or misconfiguration.
  • Clearer device topology: Interfaces without IPs now appear distinctly in your maps, so physical connectivity is easier to interpret and troubleshoot.
  • Smoother billing and signup flows: Faster plan selection and payment with integrated Stripe card entry, plus DNS checks to ensure your organization always gets Scanopy alerts.
  • Enhanced org-level security: Only managers can create or edit credentials and tags, protecting your setup from accidental changes.
